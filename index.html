Apa itu Subject-Verb Agreement?

Simply put: Singular Subject must have a Singular Verb, and Plural Subject must have a Plural Verb! Mereka harus "akur" atau sejalan!

Singular (Tunggal)

1 Person/Thing ➔ Verb pakai -s/-es (Present) atau was/is/has

Example: The student walks to school.

Plural (Jamak)

>1 Person/Thing ➔ Verb tanpa -s atau were/are/have

Example: The students walk to school.

Aturan Utama TOEFL (Structure Rules)

Diambil dari panduan TOEFL PBT (Section 2 - Structure & Written Expression):

Rule 1: Prepositional Phrases (Jebakan Pengganggu!)

Kata di antara Subject dan Verb (seperti of, in, with, on, along with) BUKAN Subject!

The key (to the doors) is missing. (Subject = key [Singular])

Rule 2: Expressions of Quantity (Some, All, Most)

Subject tergantung kata benda di dalam frasa "of...":

Most of the book is interesting. (Uncountable/Singular)
Most of the books are interesting. (Plural)

Rule 3: Inverted Subjects (There / Here & Prepositions)

Jika kalimat dimulai dengan There/Here atau frasa tempat, Subject ada di BELAKANG Verb!

There are three tests today. (Subject = tests)

Contoh Soal TOEFL

Example 1 (TOEFL Style):

The articles in this magazine ______ very informative for high school students.

A) is     B) was     C) are     D) has been

Penjelasan: Subject kalimat adalah "The articles" (Plural), bukan "magazine". Maka butuh Verb jamak yaitu are.
